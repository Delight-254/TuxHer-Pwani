# 🌊 TuxHer Pwani

### Empowering women. Building skills. Shaping the future of technology on the Kenyan Coast.

**TuxHer Pwani** is a community-focused digital initiative designed to encourage, connect and empower girls and women interested in technology across Kenya's coastal region.

Technology needs more women at the table. TuxHer Pwani is about creating a space where curiosity is welcomed, skills are developed, opportunities are shared and more girls can see themselves building the future of tech.

> The future of technology is not complete without women.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make technology more accessible, inclusive and welcoming for girls and women on the Kenyan coast by encouraging digital skills development, community connections and participation in the tech ecosystem.

## ✨ What You'll Find

- **Community:** A space that encourages women and girls to connect through technology.
- **Learning pathways:** An introduction to different areas of technology and opportunities to explore them.
- **Digital empowerment:** Encouraging confidence, creativity and practical technology skills.
- **Tech-inspired experience:** A terminal-inspired visual section celebrating the world of computing and Linux.
- **Get involved:** A section designed to welcome people interested in joining the initiative.

## 💻 Built With

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Google Fonts
- Responsive web design

The current website uses custom CSS styling, responsive layouts and a coastal-inspired visual identity.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

TuxHer Pwani combines technology culture with coastal identity.

Its visual language draws inspiration from:
- 🌊 Ocean-inspired turquoise.
- 🌸 Coastal-inspired colours and patterns.
- 💻 Terminal aesthetics and hacker culture.
- ✨ A welcoming, modern interface.
