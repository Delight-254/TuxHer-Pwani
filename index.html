Tech is better when we all have root access.

  

    
The name says it: Tux, the Linux penguin, and Pwani, Swahili for the coast. We are women who code, design and defend, based along the Kenyan coast and open to anyone who wants to learn.

    
You do not need a degree or years of experience. Beginners sit next to professionals, ask the questions, and leave with something built.

  






  
Three tracks, one community.

  

    
Cybersecurity

      
Learn to think like an attacker so you can defend like a pro. Practice on safe labs, join capture-the-flag nights, and learn the basics of Linux, networks and staying safe online.
nmap, wireshark, ctf, linux

    
Web development

      
Build and ship real websites, from your first HTML page to full apps. Pair up, review each other’s code, and launch projects for local businesses and causes.
html, css, javascript, git

    
App design

      
Design apps that people actually enjoy using. Sketch, prototype and test with real users, then hand off designs that developers can build.
ux research, figma, prototypes
